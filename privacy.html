Privacy Policy

Tonami does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

What Tonami does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sent to us or to any third party.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (key, sound, tempo and so on) are saved only on your device.
  • Recordings are saved only on your device, or wherever you choose to export them.

Local network and nearby devices

If you turn on Send MIDI to Mac or host a Jam, Tonami connects directly to your own devices nearby (over your local network, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) to send musical notes between them. These connections stay between your devices. Tonami asks for local network permission only for this.

Children

Tonami collects no information from anyone, including children, so it is suitable for classrooms.
